Ideas Behind The Site:
I created this site as an escape from more mainstream social media.
I also wanted to challenge myself to learn something new in HTML and CSS coding, without as much help from big-name website building companies.
The main purpose of the site was a minimalist, calm website to host my architectural drawings, share things about my architectural library, and just whatever else seemed fun to share. A journal really.

Everything is pretty basic HTML. The whole site is static and the pages live on my computer. Hosting is through GIThub.

I have some plans for the future to add sections and some interesting graphical items having to do with maps...but for now I'm keeping it simple.
